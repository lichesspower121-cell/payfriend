FAQ

Is PayFriend free to use?

Yes. Using the bot is free. You earn from referrals, tasks, and the daily reward.

Do I have to complete tasks?

No. Tasks are optional. You can earn from referrals and the daily reward only.

How do withdrawals work?

Reach the minimum balance, tap Withdraw, enter your gWallet ID. An admin pays you and marks the request as paid in the bot.

Where can I get help?

Use Support in the bot menu or join the support group.
